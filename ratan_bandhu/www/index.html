Ratan Bandhu Fabrics Pvt Ltd

A Surat fabric house, in its thirty-second year.

The company was incorporated on 15 March 1994 and is registered with the Registrar of Companies, Ahmedabad. Its licensed activity is the preparation and spinning of textile fibre, including the weaving of textiles — NIC code 1711 — and it operates from the Mahaveer Textile Market on Ring Road, Surat.

Work is organised the way the market works: goods move in catalogs, buyers pick across catalogs, and the order is settled in conversation. This website keeps that rhythm and only removes the part nobody enjoys — sending photos one by one and re-typing the design numbers.
